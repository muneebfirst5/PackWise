Why use a travel packing list?

Packing for a trip can become stressful when you have to remember clothing, toiletries, travel documents, electronics and everyday essentials. A travel packing list gives you one place to organize everything before you leave.

Create a personalized vacation packing list

Every trip is different. A five-day beach vacation requires different items from a winter hiking trip or an international business trip. PackWise lets you enter your trip details and create a checklist that matches your journey.

Check your packing before you travel

Use the interactive checklist to mark items as packed. You can also save your trip in your browser or print the checklist before leaving home.

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers about PackWise.

What is PackWise?

PackWise is a travel packing list generator designed to help you organize the items you may need for your trip.

How does the packing list generator work?

Enter your home location, destination, number of days, travelers, trip type and expected weather. PackWise then creates a checklist based on those details.

Can I save my packing list?

Yes. You can save your trip in your browser and access saved trips from the generator.

Can I print my packing list?

Yes. After creating your list, use the Print List button to print your checklist.

Is PackWise mobile friendly?

Yes. The website is designed to work on phones, tablets and desktop computers.
