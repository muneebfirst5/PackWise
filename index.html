Common packing questions

Packing depends on where you are going, how long you will stay and what you plan to do.

What should I pack for a vacation?

Start with clothing, toiletries, documents, electronics and any items specific to your destination or activities.

How much should I pack for five days?

A five-day trip usually needs fewer clothes than a longer trip, but the right amount depends on your activities and access to laundry.

What should I pack for cold weather?

Layers, warm clothing, suitable footwear and weather protection are important starting points for colder destinations.

What should I pack for a beach trip?

Consider swimwear, light clothing, sun protection, toiletries and items you will use around the water.

What should I pack for hiking?

Hiking trips usually require suitable footwear, comfortable clothing, water and basic outdoor essentials.

What should I pack for international travel?

In addition to normal travel items, check your passport, required documents, payment methods and destination-specific requirements.

Why use a travel packing list?

Packing for a trip can be easy to put off until the last minute. A simple checklist gives you one place to organize the things you need before you leave.

Make a packing list around your trip

A weekend city trip is different from a week-long beach vacation or a hiking trip. The clothes, equipment and everyday items you need can change depending on the destination and activities.

PackWise lets you enter your trip details and create a checklist based on them. You can then check items as you pack instead of trying to remember everything at the last minute.

Use the checklist before you travel

Once your list is ready, go through it before leaving home. Check important documents, clothing, toiletries, electronics and other essentials. You can also save the list in your browser or print it.
