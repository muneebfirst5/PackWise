Carry-On Packing List

Packing only a carry-on bag? This practical checklist covers clothing, toiletries, electronics, documents and other essentials that can help you travel light.

Clothing

  • 2–4 versatile shirts or tops
  • 1–2 pairs of pants, shorts or skirts
  • Underwear for the length of your trip
  • Socks
  • Sleepwear
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light jacket or sweater if needed

Toiletries

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Small toiletries
  • Deodorant
  • Hairbrush or comb
  • Skincare products
  • Personal-care items

Electronics

  • Phone
  • Phone charger
  • Power bank
  • Headphones or earbuds
  • Travel adapter if needed

Travel Documents

  • Passport or identification
  • Visa or entry documents if required
  • Boarding pass or transportation details
  • Accommodation details
  • Travel insurance information

Other Carry-On Essentials

  • Wallet and payment cards
  • Sunglasses
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Small travel pouch
  • Any important medications or personal items

Pack Light

A carry-on works best when you choose versatile clothing and avoid packing items you are unlikely to use. Check your airline's current baggage rules and restrictions before your trip.

Tips for Packing a Carry-On Bag

Pack only what you need and choose versatile clothing that works for different activities. Before travelling, check your airline's carry-on size, weight and liquid restrictions.

  • Choose lightweight clothing that can be mixed and matched.
  • Keep your passport, boarding pass and important documents accessible.
  • Use small containers for toiletries and follow airport liquid rules.
  • Keep essential medication and valuable items in your carry-on.
  • Place chargers and electronics where you can reach them easily.
  • Leave some space for items you may need during your journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I pack in my carry-on bag?

Start with travel documents, phone, charger, essential medication, toiletries that meet airport rules, a change of clothes and any items you need during the journey.

Can I bring toiletries in my carry-on?

Many airports restrict liquids, gels and aerosols in carry-on baggage. Check the current rules for your departure airport and airline before packing.

How can I fit more items into a carry-on?

Choose versatile clothing, avoid unnecessary duplicates and use packing cubes or small organizers to make better use of your available space.
