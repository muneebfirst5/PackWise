Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Welcome to PackWise. PackWise is a free travel planning tool that helps users create personalized packing lists based on trip information and weather conditions.

Information We Collect

PackWise does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, or password.

Saved Trips

PackWise can save trip information using your browser's local storage. This information is stored locally on your device through your browser and is not stored in a PackWise account or database.

You can clear this locally stored information by clearing your browser's site data or using the available saved-trip controls provided by the website.

Weather Information

PackWise uses weather information to help create packing recommendations. When you request weather information, your destination may be sent through the weather service used by PackWise in order to retrieve current weather data.

Cookies and Analytics

PackWise does not currently use Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager based on the website configuration at the time this policy was published.

Your browser may still store information such as local storage data required for features such as Saved Trips.

Third-Party Services

PackWise may use third-party services to provide functionality such as weather information and website hosting. Those services may process technical information necessary to provide their services.

Data Security

Because Saved Trips are stored locally in your browser, PackWise does not maintain a central database of those saved trips.

Children's Privacy

PackWise is a general-purpose travel planning website and is not specifically directed toward children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PackWise's features or services change. Any updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PackWise, please use the contact information provided on the PackWise website, when available.
