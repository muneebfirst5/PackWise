One Week Packing List: 7-Day Travel Checklist

Planning a one-week trip? This practical one week packing list helps you organize clothing, toiletries, electronics, travel documents and other travel essentials for a 7-day trip.

Clothing

  • 5–7 shirts or tops
  • 2–3 pairs of pants, shorts or skirts
  • 7 pairs of underwear
  • 7 pairs of socks
  • Sleepwear
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light jacket or sweater
  • Weather-appropriate clothing

Toiletries

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Shampoo and body wash
  • Deodorant
  • Hairbrush or comb
  • Skincare products
  • Personal-care items you regularly use

Electronics

  • Phone
  • Phone charger
  • Power bank
  • Travel adapter if needed
  • Headphones or earbuds

Travel Documents

  • Passport or identification
  • Visa or entry documents if required
  • Flight or transportation details
  • Hotel or accommodation details
  • Travel insurance information, if applicable

Other Useful Items

  • Small day bag
  • Sunglasses
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Small laundry bag
  • Destination-specific items

Pack According to the Weather

Your ideal packing list depends on your destination, activities and the weather. Check the forecast before your trip and adjust your clothing and footwear accordingly.

Tips for Packing for a 7-Day Trip

For a one-week trip, choose versatile clothes that can be mixed and matched. Plan around your activities and consider whether laundry will be available during your stay.

  • Pack enough underwear and socks for your plans.
  • Choose clothing that works in multiple outfit combinations.
  • Check the weather forecast before choosing extra layers.
  • Keep travel documents and valuables secure and accessible.
  • Use packing cubes or separate bags to organize belongings.
  • Leave some space for souvenirs or items bought during your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I pack for a one-week trip?

Start with clothing, underwear, toiletries, electronics, travel documents and comfortable shoes. Add items based on your destination, weather and planned activities.

How many clothes should I pack for a 7-day trip?

A useful starting point is one outfit per day, along with underwear, socks and sleepwear. Adjust the quantities based on your activities, weather and access to laundry.

Can I pack for a week in a carry-on bag?

Yes, it may be possible with versatile clothing and compact toiletries. Check your airline's baggage dimensions and liquid restrictions before travelling.

What should I pack for a week-long trip in rainy weather?

Consider a waterproof jacket, suitable footwear, quick-drying clothing and a bag to protect electronics and important documents from moisture.
