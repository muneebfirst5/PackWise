One Week Packing List: 7-Day Travel Checklist

Planning a one-week trip? This simple packing checklist covers clothing, toiletries, electronics, documents and other travel essentials.

Create My Personalized Packing List

Clothing

  • 5–7 shirts or tops
  • 2–3 pairs of pants, shorts or skirts
  • 7 pairs of underwear
  • 7 pairs of socks
  • Sleepwear
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light jacket or sweater
  • Weather-appropriate clothing

Toiletries

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Shampoo and body wash
  • Deodorant
  • Hairbrush or comb
  • Skincare products
  • Any regular personal-care items

Electronics

  • Phone
  • Phone charger
  • Power bank
  • Travel adapter if needed
  • Headphones or earbuds

Travel Documents

  • Passport or identification
  • Visa or entry documents if required
  • Flight or transportation details
  • Hotel or accommodation details
  • Travel insurance information

Other Useful Items

  • Small day bag
  • Sunglasses
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Small laundry bag
  • Any destination-specific items

Pack According to the Weather

Tips for Packing for a 7-Day Trip

For a one-week trip, plan your clothes around the weather and activities at your destination. Choose versatile items that can be mixed and matched, and consider whether laundry will be available during your stay.

  • Pack enough underwear and socks for your plans.
  • Choose clothing that works in multiple outfit combinations.
  • Check the weather forecast before deciding on jackets or extra layers.
  • Keep travel documents, essential medication and valuables accessible.
  • Use packing cubes or separate bags to organize your belongings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many clothes should I pack for a 7-day trip?

A useful starting point is one outfit per day, along with underwear, socks and sleepwear. Adjust your quantities based on activities, weather and access to laundry.

Can I pack for a week in a carry-on bag?

Yes, it may be possible with versatile clothing and compact toiletries. Check your airline's baggage dimensions and restrictions before travelling.

What should I pack for a week-long trip in rainy weather?

Consider a waterproof jacket, suitable footwear, quick-drying clothing and a bag to protect electronics and important documents from moisture.
