Beach Vacation Packing List: What to Pack

Planning a beach vacation? This beach vacation packing list helps you prepare swimwear, sun protection, toiletries, travel documents and other essentials. Use this checklist to stay organized and enjoy your trip without forgetting important items.

1. Beach Clothing and Footwear

  • Swimsuits or swim trunks
  • Lightweight shirts and shorts
  • Underwear and sleepwear
  • Beach cover-up or lightweight dress
  • Comfortable sandals or flip-flops
  • Walking shoes for sightseeing
  • One light jacket or sweater for cooler evenings
  • Extra outfit for restaurants or evening activities

2. Sun Protection and Beach Essentials

  • Broad-spectrum sunscreen, ideally SPF 30 or higher
  • Sunglasses with UV protection
  • Wide-brimmed hat or cap
  • Beach towel
  • Beach bag or waterproof tote
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Waterproof phone pouch
  • Book, cards or other beach entertainment
  • Reusable bag for wet swimwear

3. Toiletries and Personal Care

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Shampoo and conditioner
  • Body wash or soap
  • Deodorant
  • Hairbrush or comb
  • Moisturizer and lip balm
  • Personal medications and basic first-aid supplies
  • After-sun moisturizer, if needed

4. Travel Documents and Money

  • Passport or other required identification
  • Tickets and boarding passes
  • Hotel or accommodation confirmation
  • Travel insurance details, if applicable
  • Debit or credit cards and some cash
  • Copies of important travel documents

5. Electronics and Useful Extras

  • Mobile phone and charger
  • Power bank
  • Camera, if needed
  • Headphones
  • Travel adapter for your destination
  • Small daypack for outings
  • Snacks for the journey

6. Tips for Packing for a Beach Vacation

  • Check the weather forecast before choosing your clothes.
  • Pack lightweight clothing that can be mixed and matched.
  • Bring enough swimwear for your trip length and drying time.
  • Keep sunscreen and essential items easy to reach.
  • Check airline baggage rules and liquid restrictions before flying.
  • Check local swimming conditions and follow beach safety advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I pack for a beach vacation?

Start with swimwear, lightweight clothes, sandals, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, toiletries, travel documents and your phone charger. Add items based on your destination and planned activities.

How many swimsuits should I bring?

For a short trip, two swimsuits are often practical so one can dry while you use the other. For longer trips, adjust the number to your activities and laundry options.

Should I bring a beach towel?

Check whether your hotel or accommodation provides towels. If not, pack one, preferably a lightweight towel that dries quickly.

How can I avoid overpacking for a beach trip?

Plan outfits in advance, choose versatile clothing, and pack only what you expect to use. Check your luggage allowance before traveling.
