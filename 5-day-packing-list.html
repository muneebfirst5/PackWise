5-Day Travel Packing List

Planning a five-day trip? This simple packing checklist covers clothing, toiletries, electronics, documents and other travel essentials.

Clothing

  • 4–5 shirts or tops
  • 2 pairs of pants, shorts or skirts
  • 5 pairs of underwear
  • 5 pairs of socks
  • Sleepwear
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light jacket or sweater if needed
  • Weather-appropriate clothing

Toiletries

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Shampoo and body wash
  • Deodorant
  • Hairbrush or comb
  • Skincare products
  • Personal-care items

Electronics

  • Phone
  • Phone charger
  • Power bank
  • Travel adapter if needed
  • Headphones or earbuds

Travel Documents

  • Passport or identification
  • Visa or entry documents if required
  • Flight or transportation details
  • Hotel or accommodation details
  • Travel insurance information

Other Useful Items

  • Small day bag
  • Sunglasses
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Small laundry bag
  • Destination-specific items

Tips for Packing for a 5-Day Trip

For a five-day trip, plan outfits around your activities and expected weather. Choose versatile clothing that can be worn in different combinations, and adjust quantities to suit your needs.

  • Pack around five days of underwear and socks.
  • Choose tops and bottoms that can be mixed and matched.
  • Check the weather forecast before selecting outerwear.
  • Keep important documents and essential medication accessible.
  • Use a small laundry bag to separate worn clothes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many outfits should I pack for a 5-day trip?

A practical starting point is one outfit per day, plus sleepwear and suitable outerwear. You may need more or fewer clothes depending on your activities and access to laundry.

Can I pack for five days in a carry-on?

Often, yes. Choose versatile clothes, limit toiletries to what you need, and check your airline's baggage size and liquid restrictions before travelling.

What should I pack for a five-day trip in cold weather?

Consider warm layers, a suitable jacket or coat, warm socks, comfortable footwear and any weather protection needed for your destination.
