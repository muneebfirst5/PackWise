10-Day Travel Packing List

Planning a ten-day trip? This simple packing checklist covers clothing, toiletries, electronics, documents and other travel essentials.

Clothing

  • 7–10 shirts or tops
  • 3–4 pairs of pants, shorts or skirts
  • 10 pairs of underwear
  • 10 pairs of socks
  • Sleepwear
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light jacket or sweater
  • Weather-appropriate clothing

Toiletries

  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Shampoo and body wash
  • Deodorant
  • Hairbrush or comb
  • Skincare products
  • Personal-care items

Electronics

  • Phone
  • Phone charger
  • Power bank
  • Travel adapter if needed
  • Headphones or earbuds

Travel Documents

  • Passport or identification
  • Visa or entry documents if required
  • Flight or transportation details
  • Hotel or accommodation details
  • Travel insurance information

Other Useful Items

  • Small day bag
  • Sunglasses
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Small laundry bag
  • Destination-specific items

Tips for Packing for a 10-Day Trip

For a ten-day trip, plan your clothing around your destination, activities and expected weather. Choose versatile items that can be mixed and matched, and consider doing laundry during your stay.

  • Choose clothes that work in multiple outfit combinations.
  • Check the weather forecast before packing jackets or extra layers.
  • Pack enough underwear and socks, or plan to do laundry.
  • Keep travel documents, essential medication and valuables accessible.
  • Use packing cubes or separate bags to organize your belongings.
  • Check airline baggage limits before leaving for the airport.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many outfits should I pack for a 10-day trip?

A useful starting point is one outfit per day, but versatile clothing and access to laundry can help you pack less. Adjust your choices for your activities and the weather.

Can I pack for 10 days in a carry-on bag?

It may be possible if you choose versatile clothes, compact toiletries and only the essentials. Check your airline's baggage dimensions and restrictions before travelling.

What should I pack for a 10-day trip in cold weather?

Consider warm layers, a suitable coat, warm socks, comfortable shoes and weather protection appropriate for your destination.
